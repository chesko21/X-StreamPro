# Privacy Policy for X-StreamPro

**Last Updated: October 2023**

Chesko Dev Team ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by X-StreamPro.

## 1. Information We Collect
X-StreamPro is a client-side application for streaming IPTV content. 
- **User Credentials**: When you log in, your Server URL, Username, and Password are used only to authenticate with your chosen IPTV provider.
- **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) on our servers. All login data is stored locally on your device for session management.

## 2. Permissions
To provide its services, the app may request the following permissions:
- **Internet**: Required to stream content from your IPTV provider.
- **Access Network State**: Used to check connectivity status.
- **Post Notifications**: Used for service-related notifications (if applicable).

## 3. Third-Party Services
X-StreamPro interacts with third-party IPTV servers provided by the user. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party providers. Please review their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Picture-in-Picture (PiP)
The app uses system-level PiP features. This feature does not collect any data; it only provides an overlay for media playback.

## 5. Security
We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you provide within the application. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website: [https://chesko-25.vercel.app/](https://chesko-25.vercel.app/)
